How it works
  1. Each ID becomes an email (ANDT14 → ANDT14@fpt.com). Duplicates are struck through and invalid entries shown in red; both are skipped.
  2. “Start group chat” opens a new Teams chat with everyone in the To field, plus your group name and first message.
  3. Check the people count in Teams, then press Send. The group is created at that moment.
  4. You don’t need to include yourself. Teams group chats hold up to 250 people.
